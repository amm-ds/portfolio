# Projects

Ten showcases across agentic AI, classical ML, deep learning, optimization,
model serving, decision science, and large-scale open-data engineering. Each
page links the problem, the design record, and the evaluation artifacts; the
same case studies render with interactive figures in the showcase app when it
runs locally (`make docker-up` → http://localhost:8000, Projects section).
Public deployment status: [deploy/DEPLOY.md](https://github.com/amm-ds/portfolio/blob/main/deploy/DEPLOY.md).

| # | Project | Domain | Data | Impact in one line | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [RAG & Agentic AI Assistant](01-rag-agent.md) | Agentic AI / LLM | Wikipedia (HF) | A question-answering assistant that answers only from a pinned 593-article Wikipedia corpus and cites every claim. Retrieval finds the right passage 83% of the time, but 18 of 65 hosted answers kept unsupported claims—answers are auditable, yet not trustworthy unattended.<br>Pinned: retrieval recall@5 0.83 vs 0.03 random; verify loop cut unsupported-claim share 60%→47% in an on/off A/B (committed hosted 65-q run: 18/65) | Flagship — complete |
| 02 | [Demand Forecasting](02-demand-forecasting.md) | Time series | Walmart M5 subset (primary) + UCI Retail II (sparse secondary) + synthetic CI path | One honest protocol, two demand regimes: on dense Walmart M5 daily sales a tuned ensemble cuts forecast error to 49% vs 60% for the seasonal-naive baseline; on sparse 89%-zero retail demand the winner is the opposite—mostly-zero routing for 945 of 1,069 lumpy series.<br>Pinned: M5 ensemble WAPE 0.491 vs 0.603 naive (gap CI excludes zero); UCI uses conservative selective routing, with zero for 945 of 1,069 lumpy series | Working showcase |
| 03 | [Anomaly Detection over Telemetry](03-anomaly-detection.md) | Unsupervised ML | NAB (real) + seeded synthetic CI path | On NAB at a fixed 2% alert budget, IsolationForest catches 16 of 17 labelled incident windows but fires 1,609 alerts. Deduplication plus a one-page-per-day cap cuts that to 80 pages at modest recall cost—page the alert stream, not the detector score.<br>Pinned: 16 of 17 labelled windows touched at 1,609 alerts — episode dedup + a 24 h page cap cuts pages to 80 and raises range-F1 0.157 → 0.315; train-side operating-point tuning halves IsolationForest pages again (80 → 46, range-F1 0.315 → 0.382) while OCSVM's marginal adoption fails to transfer — the measured limit of 6-window validation | Working showcase |
| 04 | [NLP Classification with Transformers](04-nlp-classification.md) | Deep learning NLP | AG News (HF, full 120k) | News sorter for AG News' four topics: a language model trained on all 120k articles scores 95% macro-F1 on a fresh holdout, vs 88% for a keyword model 40x faster and free; routing doubtful items to it keeps that quality while 3 of 4 items ride at keyword cost. License unknown.<br>Pinned: full-data fine-tune reaches 0.946 macro-F1 on a laptop GPU; a controlled same-device ladder isolates the data effect (+1.3–1.6 pp per 3× rows) from device noise (≤0.011 macro-F1) | Working showcase |
| 05 | [Churn Modeling with sklearn Pipelines](05-ml-pipeline-churn.md) | Classical ML | IBM telco churn (real) + synthetic CI path | A churn scorer on real IBM telco data (7,043 customers): serving the calibrated model at a 0.175 threshold cuts decision cost 42% (601 vs 1,035 for contacting everyone; 95% bootstrap interval -485 to -343). The cutoff must be re-derived when a missed churner's price changes.<br>Pinned: cuts retention-campaign cost 41.9% vs the naive contact-everyone policy (CI excludes zero); the cutoff moves with the missed-churner price (t\*=0.200/0.175/0.100 at FN=3/5/8) | Working showcase |
| 06 | [Routing Optimization (VRPTW)](06-routing-optimization.md) | Combinatorial optimization | CVRPLIB Solomon | Routing planner for the 56 standard Solomon 100-customer problems with time windows. A hand-written search returns feasible plans within 4.2% of best-known routes in ~1 second each, using 3.7 more vehicles than best-known on average; an exact solver certifies small cases only.<br>Pinned: within 4.23% of best-known solutions on all 56 instances (+3.73 vehicles); an unhinted CP-SAT at the same 10 s budget finds no feasible plan on any instance | Working showcase |
| 07 | [Urban Mobility Demand Intelligence](07-open-data-mobility.md) | Data engineering + ML | NYC TLC + NOAA | Next-hour NYC taxi demand by zone, built on 41.2M real 2024 yellow-taxi trips and public weather: on a strict Oct-Dec holdout the model was 28% more accurate than same-hour-yesterday; its 90% upper band reached 91% coverage (sparse zones excepted).<br>Pinned: served tuned GBM: −28.3% demand error on 41.2M trips; p90 staffing band covers 90.8%; the first unseen drift month (2025-01) scores in-window at −5.0%, and green-taxi demand serves from a per-service retrained bundle routed by an optional API `service` field | **Flagship — complete** |
| 08 | [LLM Inference & Serving](08-inference-serving.md) | Model serving | Local Ollama stack (GGUF vs MLX) | One laptop, one run: five LLM serving setups, 760 requests, zero failures. A 4-bit mixture-of-experts model answers in 0.81 s per request at about $0.12 per 1M output tokens (electricity only). The reusable asset is the harness for re-measuring any stack on target hardware.<br>Pinned: measured latency/throughput frontier across engines, quants, concurrency | Working showcase |
| 09 | [Agentic AI Workflows](09-agentic-workflows.md) | Agentic AI | Synthetic 12-doc "Atlas Valley" handbook + read-only Wikipedia slice from 01 (CC BY-SA) | An auditable plan-act-review AI workflow with fixed tools and budgets passed all automated checks: 15/15 fixed tasks, 13/13 unseen answers, 5/6 should-fail probes, ~$0.13 per suite. Ready for supervised pilots, not unattended use. Data: synthetic handbook, real Wikipedia chunks.<br>Pinned: 15/15 on every grader on a hosted model + held-out 13/13 answers, 5/6 should-fail refusals (n=19 suite) — suite $0.13 of the $2.00 cap, cloud-equivalent; the local-provider wave meters $0.00 but breaks the hosted-tuned 60 s budget on 7/9 tasks | Working showcase |
| 10 | [Retention Uplift & Experimentation](10-uplift-experimentation.md) | Decision Science | Hillstrom email (MineThatData) | On 64,000-customer randomized email data, uplift targeting beat random targeting by +$2,812 (+69%) at a 25% send budget, CI [+$718, +$4,767]; at $0.01 per email, mailing everyone still wins. Targeting pays when sending is costly. Data: Hillstrom MineThatData, March 2008.<br>Pinned: targeting profit advantage established by paired bootstrap; mail-everyone named as the bar | Working showcase |
