# Contact

**Open to freelance and consulting engagements** — typical response within
48 hours. Languages: English, Spanish. Timezone: flexible overlap with EU
and US hours.

## Start a conversation

The fastest route is a **GitHub inquiry** — it opens a tracked thread I can
move to email or a call from:

→ [Open an inquiry](https://github.com/amm-ds/portfolio/issues/new?title=Inquiry%3A%20&body=%2A%2AContext%2A%2A%3A%20%28what%20you%27re%20building%20or%20deciding%29%0A%2A%2ATimeline%2A%2A%3A%20%0A%2A%2AWhat%20done%20looks%20like%2A%2A%3A%20)
(include a sentence of context and I reply with options and rough scope)

Or find me at **[github.com/amm-ds](https://github.com/amm-ds)** — all work
is public and reproducible.

## How engagements start

A free 30–45 minute discovery call — problem, data reality, constraints, and
what "done" must look like — followed by a written summary with options and
rough scope. See [Services](services.md) for offerings, process, and
engagement models.
