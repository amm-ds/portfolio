Business insights

  • Rain suppresses curb demand: the wettest-borough ratio is EWR at 1.080x dry-hour demand, the most resilient is N/A at 0.895x.
  • p90 uncertainty band calibration: 90.8% of realised zone-hours fall at or below the quantile-GBM upper band (target 90%) — usable for fleet/staffing buffers.
  • Best model by MAE: gbm_xgb_tuned (4.487 pickups/hour MAE city-wide).
  • Caveat: rain/dry ratios are raw groupbys — hour-of-week mix is not normalised; treat them as directional elasticity, not causal estimates.
