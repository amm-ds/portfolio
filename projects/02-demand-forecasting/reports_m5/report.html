Demand forecasting on M5

Introduction

This report evaluates demand forecasting on the M5 Walmart subset: 600 daily store-item series (CA_1, CA_3, TX_2), 2011-01-29 → 2016-06-19, 1,105,594 rows, 16.4% zero-demand days — the dense-demand regime where ML forecasting demonstrably beats seasonal baselines. Protocol m5_direct_v1: 10 weekly rolling origins (8 selection + 2 scored-once test), horizon 28 days, min training history 1096 days; every number below is computed from the committed JSON artifacts at build time.

Business insights

  • Forecast value-add: the convex ensemble cuts pooled test WAPE from 0.6032 (seasonal_naive) to 0.4911 — a 18.6% reduction; the paired cluster bootstrap puts the gap at -0.1121 (95% CI [-0.1224, -0.1021], significant).
  • Safety stock from the band: the per-series scaled split-conformal 80% band covers 77.9% of test actuals (target 80%) at mean width 13.41 units (lgbm: 77.9% at 13.53, dense 77.2% / intermittent 80.4%) — calibrated on selection origins only, so it is usable as a replenishment buffer width per horizon day.
  • Intermittent-regime caveat: the win holds in both volume classes (dense WAPE 0.4638, intermittent 0.6037), but the margin per series is thinner there and the band runs slightly wide — size intermittent safety stock from the band, not from the point forecast alone.
  • Tuning/ensemble evidence: the lgbm config is the best of a seeded 30-trial Optuna study on the selection origins (params.yaml ``m5_lgbm``), and the ensemble (lgbm x 0.45, auto_ets x 0.45, auto_ces x 0.10) is grid-fit on a 0.05 simplex over the same origins — neither saw the test windows.
  • Caveat: two scored-once test origins on one 600-series subset of M5; the gaps are statistically established on this panel but should not be read as universal effect sizes.

Protocol

Origins (weekly, 7-day spacing; the last two are scored once as TEST): selection ['2016-03-15', '2016-03-22', '2016-03-29', '2016-04-05', '2016-04-12', '2016-04-19', '2016-04-26', '2016-05-03'] | test ['2016-05-10', '2016-05-17']; horizon 28 days, min training history 1096 days.

Candidates

  • seasonal_naive — per-series seasonal random walk (m=7) — the floor
  • window_avg_28 — per-series mean of the last 28 days (statsforecast)
  • adida — per-series aggregation-disaggregation intermittent demand (statsforecast)
  • tsb — per-series Teunter-Syntetos-Babai smoothing (alpha_d=alpha_p=0.1)
  • auto_ets — per-series automatic ETS model selection (season_length=7)
  • auto_ces — per-series automatic complex exponential smoothing (season_length=7)
  • lgbm — global LightGBM over all 600 series (mlforecast recursive; lags 1/7/14/21/28, rolling means/stds, expanding mean, calendar + price/SNAP/event exog; wave-25 tuned config)
  • convex_ensemble — convex blend of the best three of {lgbm, auto_ets, auto_ces} by selection WAPE (simplex grid step 0.05, selection origins only)

Ensemble rule: best three of the pool by selection WAPE; weights simplex-grid-fit on selection origins only (step 0.05, ties -> first grid point); committed members lgbm, auto_ets, auto_ces.

Metric definitions

  • mae: mean |actual - forecast| over the scored (series x day) cells
  • wape: sum |actual - forecast| / sum actual over the scored cells (pooled volume-weighted absolute percentage error)
  • rmae: candidate pooled MAE / seasonal_naive pooled MAE over the same cells (< 1 beats the naive floor); a zero naive MAE floors the ratio at 1.0
  • wrmsse: per (series x origin) unit: sqrt(MSE over the 28 scored days / in-sample MSE of the one-step seasonal-naive-28 over the series' training window); zero-denominator units excluded; 'wrmsse' = mean over units, 'wrmsse_weighted' = weighted by the training-window volume. NOTE: the official M5 denominator is the lag-1 random walk; this track uses the seasonal-naive-28 denominator per the wave-24 plan (a stricter scale for weekly-seasonal dense demand)
  • peak_mae: MAE on the window's top-decile-by-actual days (k = ceil(0.1*28) = 3)
  • win_rate_vs_naive: share of series whose pooled test |error| is below the seasonal_naive pooled test |error|
  • pinball: mean pinball loss at the stated quantile over the test cells
  • coverage_80: share of test actuals inside the per-series scaled split-conformal 80% band (pooled and by volume class)
