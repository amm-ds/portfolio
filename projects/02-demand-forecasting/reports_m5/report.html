Demand forecasting on M5

Introduction

This report evaluates demand forecasting on the M5 Walmart subset: 600 daily store-item series (CA_1, CA_3, TX_2), 2011-01-29 → 2016-06-19, 1,105,594 rows, 16.4% zero-demand days — the dense-demand regime where ML forecasting demonstrably beats seasonal baselines. Protocol m5_direct_v1: 10 weekly rolling origins (8 selection + 2 scored-once test), horizon 28 days, min training history 1096 days; every number below is computed from the committed JSON artifacts at build time.

The next-week service, honestly

The headline of a demand forecast is the NEXT week — later weeks roll forward as new data lands. Pooled over h≤7 of all 10 rolling origins (each origin's first-week forecast is one deployed weekly forecast; the 8 selection origins are included, so this is the operational service number, not a held-out estimate), convex_ensemble posts next-week WAPE 0.4236 (rmae 0.778) against the seasonal_naive floor's 0.5442; lgbm is at 0.4327 (rmae 0.795). The paired cluster bootstrap on this metric: lgbm vs naive -0.1115 (95% CI [-0.1197, -0.1039], significant); convex_ensemble vs naive -0.1206 (95% CI [-0.1286, -0.1127], significant). Beats the naive floor in every volume class at h≤7? — lgbm: yes (dense rmae 0.791, intermittent rmae 0.806); convex_ensemble: yes (dense rmae 0.774, intermittent rmae 0.791).

How the error grows week over week

The 28-day view is a rollup of four weekly services, and they are not worth the same: week 1 carries 28% of the value (the share of convex_ensemble's total absolute-error reduction vs the floor delivered in wk1, where its WAPE is 0.4140), and the error grows roughly linearly with distance — wk4 WAPE is 1.4× the wk1 number (0.5640 vs 0.4140; the naive floor degrades the same way, 1.2×). The further out the week, the more the forecast converges to the floor — which is why the next-week number above is the service headline and the 28-day pooled table is the month view.

Business insights

  • Forecast value-add: the convex ensemble cuts pooled test WAPE from 0.6032 (seasonal_naive) to 0.4913 — a 18.6% reduction; the paired cluster bootstrap puts the gap at -0.1119 (95% CI [-0.1223, -0.1019], significant).
  • Safety stock from the band: the per-series scaled split-conformal 80% band covers 77.5% of test actuals (target 80%) at mean width 13.31 units (lgbm: 77.7% at 13.53, dense 76.9% / intermittent 80.6%) — calibrated on selection origins only, so it is usable as a replenishment buffer width per horizon day.
  • Intermittent-regime caveat: the win holds in both volume classes (dense WAPE 0.4637, intermittent 0.6052), but the margin per series is thinner there and the band runs slightly wide — size intermittent safety stock from the band, not from the point forecast alone.
  • Tuning/ensemble evidence: the lgbm config is the best of a seeded 25-trial Optuna study on the selection origins (params.yaml ``m5_lgbm``), and the ensemble (xgb x 0.45, auto_ets x 0.50, lgbm x 0.05) is grid-fit on a 0.05 simplex over the same origins — neither saw the test windows.
  • L1 vs L2 forecast shape (wave 28): the flat look of the shipped curves was hypothesised to be an L1 artifact — WAPE optimisation targets the conditional median, which mean-reverts on spiky series, while the M5 competition's WRMSSE (L2) targets the conditional mean, which tracks spikes. Verification finding: LightGBM's default booster loss was already squared error — the L1 character entered through the tuner's l1 early-stopping and the WAPE selection layers — so the pure-L2 arm lgbm_l2 (L2 objective + L2-stopped tree count) is measured against lgbm (test WAPE 0.4975 vs 0.4975; WRMSSE 0.5964 vs 0.5964 — it reproduced lgbm exactly), and the WRMSSE-selected ensemble_l2 against the WAPE-selected convex_ensemble: test WAPE 0.4952 vs 0.4913, test WRMSSE 0.5978 vs 0.5969 — on this panel the WAPE-selected blend leads BOTH metrics (WAPE 0.4913 vs 0.4952, WRMSSE 0.5969 vs 0.5978) — the L2-selection hypothesis is not supported here. The overall pooled-test-WRMSSE champion is lgbm at 0.5964. The demo ships both ensemble curves for per-series inspection (they are visually near-identical: curve day-to-day variation ~5.4 units vs ~12.2 for realized demand); headline stays the WAPE-primary blend.
  • Caveat: two scored-once test origins on one 600-series subset of M5; the gaps are statistically established on this panel but should not be read as universal effect sizes.

Protocol

Origins (weekly, 7-day spacing; the last two are scored once as TEST): selection ['2016-03-15', '2016-03-22', '2016-03-29', '2016-04-05', '2016-04-12', '2016-04-19', '2016-04-26', '2016-05-03'] | test ['2016-05-10', '2016-05-17']; horizon 28 days, min training history 1096 days.

Candidates

  • seasonal_naive — per-series seasonal random walk (m=7) — the floor
  • window_avg_28 — per-series mean of the last 28 days (statsforecast)
  • adida — per-series aggregation-disaggregation intermittent demand (statsforecast)
  • tsb — per-series Teunter-Syntetos-Babai smoothing (alpha_d=alpha_p=0.1)
  • auto_ets — per-series automatic ETS model selection (season_length=7)
  • auto_ces — per-series automatic complex exponential smoothing (season_length=7)
  • lgbm — global LightGBM over all 600 series (mlforecast recursive; lags 1/7/14/21/28, rolling means/stds, expanding mean, calendar + price/SNAP/event exog; wave-25 tuned config; wave-27 feature space)
  • lgbm_l2 — global LightGBM, pure-L2 arm (wave 28): same mlforecast feature space as lgbm, objective=regression with the tree count re-derived under an L2 early-stopping pass on the selection-only nested split (the committed m5_lgbm count was chosen by an L1 rule)
  • xgb — global XGBoost over all 600 series (same mlforecast recursive feature space as lgbm; modest a-priori config — a second booster family for ensemble diversity, wave 27)
  • lgbm_tweedie — global LightGBM, tweedie arm (wave 29): same mlforecast feature space and committed m5_lgbm config, objective=tweedie at variance power 1.1 (compound-Poisson; no re-tuning)
  • lgbm_pool_store — pooled-global LightGBM (wave 29): one fit per store_id group (3), same feature pipeline and committed config, concatenated in panel order
  • lgbm_pool_storecat — pooled-global LightGBM (wave 29): one fit per (store_id, cat_id) cell (<=9), same feature pipeline and committed config
  • convex_ensemble — convex blend of the best three of {lgbm, lgbm_l2, xgb, auto_ets, auto_ces, lgbm_tweedie, lgbm_pool_store, lgbm_pool_storecat} by SELECTION WAPE (simplex grid step 0.05, selection origins only)
  • ensemble_l2 — convex blend of the best three of the same pool by SELECTION WRMSSE, weights minimising pooled selection WRMSSE (simplex grid step 0.05, selection origins only) — the L2-selected comparison blend (wave 28)

Ensemble rule: wave 30: best three of the pool by the stability screen — mean + 1.0 x population std of the per-origin pooled WAPE over the 8 selection origins; weights simplex-grid-fit (step 0.05, selection origins only, ties -> first grid point) minimising the same screen of the blend's per-origin WAPE, computed exactly from the forecast-level convex combination; pool members whose selection forecasts are bitwise identical to an admitted member are skipped (wave 28 dedup — a duplicate carries no new information and would only displace a diverse member). Replaces the wave-25..29 pooled-metric rule, whose wave-29 blend overfit selection (+0.0048 test WAPE vs wave 28); committed members xgb, auto_ets, lgbm. Wave-28 comparison blend: wave 30: the same screen on the per-origin pooled WRMSSE (zero-scale units excluded per origin) — best three of the pool ranked by mean + 1.0 x population std over the 8 selection origins, weights simplex-grid-fit minimising the same screen (step 0.05, selection origins only, ties -> first grid point); same bitwise-identity dedup as the WAPE blend — wave 28 comparison/demo material; committed members xgb, lgbm, lgbm_tweedie.

Metric definitions

  • mae: mean |actual - forecast| over the scored (series x day) cells
  • wape: sum |actual - forecast| / sum actual over the scored cells (pooled volume-weighted absolute percentage error)
  • rmae: candidate pooled MAE / seasonal_naive pooled MAE over the same cells (< 1 beats the naive floor); a zero naive MAE floors the ratio at 1.0
  • wrmsse: per (series x origin) unit: sqrt(MSE over the 28 scored days / in-sample MSE of the one-step seasonal-naive-28 over the series' training window); zero-denominator units excluded; 'wrmsse' = mean over units, 'wrmsse_weighted' = weighted by the training-window volume. NOTE: the official M5 denominator is the lag-1 random walk; this track uses the seasonal-naive-28 denominator per the wave-24 plan (a stricter scale for weekly-seasonal dense demand)
  • peak_mae: MAE on the window's top-decile-by-actual days (k = ceil(0.1*28) = 3)
  • win_rate_vs_naive: share of series whose pooled test |error| is below the seasonal_naive pooled test |error|
  • pinball: mean pinball loss at the stated quantile over the test cells
  • coverage_80: share of test actuals inside the per-series scaled split-conformal 80% band (pooled and by volume class)
  • next_week_wape: pooled WAPE over h=1..7 of EVERY rolling origin (each origin's first-week forecast is one deployed weekly forecast, so the 10 origins are 10 weekly service evaluations rolled together) — the rolling next-week service number; reported pooled, per volume class, and with the paired cluster-bootstrap gap vs seasonal_naive
  • weekly_block_wape: pooled WAPE per 7-day block of the 28-day horizon (wk1 = h1-7, wk2 = h8-14, wk3 = h15-21, wk4 = h22-28) over the 2 scored-once test origins — the honest error-growth shape of the month-ahead view
  • bias: mean(forecast - actual) / mean(actual) over the scored cells — the pooled relative over/under-forecast (positive = over-forecast); NaN when the scored volume is 0
  • stability: population std (ddof=0) of the per-origin pooled WAPE across the 8 selection origins — how steadily a candidate scores origin to origin, selection data only
  • zero_day_accuracy: share of scored points with actual == 0 whose forecast is < 0.5 (a 'quiet-day hit'); NaN when the scored block has no zero-actual points — reported pooled and for the intermittent class
  • ensemble_screen: wave-30 ensemble criterion: mean + 1.0 x population std of the per-origin pooled metric (WAPE for convex_ensemble, WRMSSE for ensemble_l2) across the 8 selection origins; ranks the pool AND fits the simplex weights (blend values computed exactly from the forecast-level convex combination, not from member metric summaries). Motivation: the M5 literature's advice to weigh fold-to-fold stability alongside average accuracy — the wave-29 mean-only (pooled) rule picked a blend +0.0048 test WAPE worse than wave 28
