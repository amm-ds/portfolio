# Example Co. Employee Handbook (extract)

## Support hours

The support team answers tickets from 09:00 to 18:00 Central European Time,
Monday through Friday. Tickets raised outside support hours are queued and
answered the next business morning. The on-call engineer handles severity-1
outages 24/7 through the pager rotation.

## Refund policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no questions
asked. After 30 days, refunds require manager approval and are prorated
against the time the subscription was active. Enterprise contracts follow the
terms of their signed agreement instead of this policy.

## Service-level agreement

Severity-1 incidents receive a first response within 1 hour and updates every
30 minutes until resolution. Severity-2 incidents receive a first response
within 4 business hours. Severity-3 questions are answered within 2 business
days.

## Escalation path

Support agents escalate severity-1 incidents directly to the on-call engineer.
All other escalations go to the shift lead first, and to the support manager
only if the shift lead cannot resolve the issue within one business day.

## Security rules

Access badges are personal and must not be shared. Laptops must lock after 5
minutes of inactivity. Customer data may only leave production systems through
the approved export pipeline, never through personal email or private
storage.
